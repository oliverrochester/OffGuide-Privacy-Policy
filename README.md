OffGuide Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

OffGuide ("the app," "we," "us") helps you learn about safety risks in the cities you visit and share information with your community. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your email address, the display name you choose, and your password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and stored only in encrypted (hashed) form. We never see your password.

Content you post. If you use community features, we store what you submit: city feed posts, comments, risk submissions, likes and dislikes, and risks you support. Your display name is shown publicly next to posts, comments and submissions.

Home city. If you set a home city, we store which city you chose so you can submit risks for it.

Reports. If you report content, we store the report (the content reported, the reason, and your account ID) so our team can review it. Reports aren't shown to other users.

Location. With your permission, OffGuide uses your device's location while the app is open to find the nearest city and show its risks, and to send you local alerts about cities you're in. Your location is processed on your device and is not sent to our servers or stored by us. You can turn off location access at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services. You can still browse cities manually without it.

Information stored only on your device. Some data never leaves your phone, including your list of blocked users and the time you've spent in a city (used to decide when to show alerts).

What we don't do

• We don't sell your personal information.
• We don't use your information for advertising.
• We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.
• The app contains no third-party analytics or advertising tools.

How we use information

We use your information to:
• create and secure your account and sign you in
• show your posts, comments and submissions to other users
• review reported content and enforce our community rules
• respond to support requests

Who we share information with

Other users can see content you post along with your display name. Your email address is never shown to other users.

Service providers. We use Supabase to host our database and handle sign-in. Supabase processes data only to provide those services to us.

Legal reasons. We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of our users or the public.

Community rules and moderation

OffGuide doesn't tolerate objectionable content or abusive behavior. You can report content or block other users from within the app. We review reports within 24 hours, may remove content that breaks our rules, and may suspend accounts that repeatedly violate them.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your account information and content for as long as your account exists. You can delete individual posts, comments and submissions at any time. You can also delete your account in the app under Profile > Delete Account, which permanently removes your profile, submissions, posts, comments, votes and reports. Deletion can't be undone.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access to stored data is restricted by database security rules. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

OffGuide isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to how it's used. Most of this is available directly in the app. For anything else, contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we'll update the effective date above and, where appropriate, let you know in the app.
